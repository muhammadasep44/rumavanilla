Direct Procurement FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this vanilla powder contain sugar, alcohol, or dextrose? +

No. Our Vanilla Powder Grade A is 100% pure cured whole vanilla beans pulverized into a fine mesh (50-150 micron). It is entirely unadulterated: zero added sugars, zero dextrose, zero maltodextrin, zero alcohol, and zero synthetic flavor enhancers.

Why does the powder have a slightly oily feel? +

Because our powder is non-exhausted (raw and never solvent-extracted), it naturally retains the rich essential lipids and vanillin oils from the cured vanilla pods and interior caviar seeds. This confirms its botanical authenticity and superior aromatic strength.

Can you produce custom packaging or white-label OEM orders? +

Yes. We offer fully customizable packaging options tailored to your exact B2B specifications, including specific pouch weights, custom labeling, barrier types, and master shipping configurations.
