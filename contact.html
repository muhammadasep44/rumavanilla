Inquiry FAQ

Common Procurement Inquiries

Quick answers to expedite your commercial quote process.

What information should be included in an RFQ?
To give you the most accurate pricing, please provide your target grade (Grade A or B), estimated quantity in kilograms or metric tons, target delivery schedule, destination airport or seaport, and whether you require FOB or CIF terms.
How long are commercial quotations valid?
Due to international ocean freight rates and harvest seasonality, our formal FOB/CIF quotations are typically valid for 14 calendar days from the date of issue. Contract prices can be locked in for annual supply agreements.
Can you accommodate third-party pre-shipment inspections?
Yes. We routinely work with internationally recognized third-party inspection agencies such as SGS, Bureau Veritas, or Intertek to verify carton counts, weights, and seal integrity prior to container dispatch.
