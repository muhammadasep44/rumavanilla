Technical FAQ

Product Questions & Answers

In-depth answers to common vanilla bean storage, quality, and usage questions.

What is vanilla frost (givre) versus mold?
White needle-like crystals on vanilla beans are often pure vanillin crystallization (known in French as givre), indicating extremely high vanillin potency. Under magnification, givre sparkles and shines like diamonds, whereas mold is dull, powdery, and lacks crystalline structure.
How should commercial clients store bulk vanilla beans?
Never refrigerate or freeze vanilla beans, as cold temperatures cause condensation and mold. Store vacuum pouches in a dark, dry pantry or staging room at 18°C to 22°C (64°F - 72°F) away from direct sunlight.
Can you supply specific moisture levels upon request?
Yes, for commercial contract orders exceeding 100kg, we can condition beans to your precise target moisture range (e.g., exactly 22% or 28%) during the final curing stage.
