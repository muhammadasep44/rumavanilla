Artisanal Curing Protocol

Our 4-Stage Curing & Grading Methodology

From green blossom harvest to sealed vacuum chamber, each bean undergoes strict artisanal care.

01

Selective Maturity Picking

Hand-harvested only when bean tips turn pale yellow, signaling peak glucovanillin accumulation in the interior cavity.

02

Hot Water Scalding & Sweating

Immersed in heated water (65°C) to stop vegetative growth, then wrapped in woolen blankets within insulated wooden sweat boxes.

03

Alternating Sun & Shade Curing

Spread onto wooden curing tables under equatorial sun for 3–4 hours daily, then transferred to shade for slow enzymatic ripening over 60 days.

04

Conditioning & Caliper Sorting

Aged in wax-paper lined conditioning trunks for 30+ days. Sorted by length with calibrated calipers and inspected pod-by-pod.

B2B Procurement Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions: Vanilla Export to Dominican Republic

Essential trade, chemical, and logistics answers compiled for commercial vanilla importers.

1. What makes Indonesian vanilla beans distinct from Madagascar Bourbon vanilla? +

Indonesian Vanilla Planifolia is renowned for its deep, smoky-bourbon, woody, and caramel flavor profile with high vanillin content (1.8% to 2.5%+). The pods are typically plumper and more oily than Madagascar beans, offering exceptional thermal stability during high-heat commercial baking and industrial extraction.

2. What are the vanillin content and moisture levels of your Grade A Planifolia beans? +

Our Grade A Vanilla Planifolia Gourmet beans are certified by third-party HPLC laboratory testing to contain 1.8% to 2.5%+ vanillin with moisture maintained between 25% and 35%. Pods are 100% whole, non-split, supple, and glistening with natural essential oils.

3. What is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) for wholesale international export? +

Our standard wholesale commercial MOQ begins at 10 kg shipped via expedited door-to-airport air cargo. For volume importers, we service commercial lots of 100 kg to 500 kg, as well as multi-ton 20ft full container loads (FCL).

4. Can commercial buyers in Dominican Republic order evaluation samples before issuing a bulk PO? +

Yes. We offer commercial evaluation sample packages (100g to 1kg) dispatched directly to Dominican Republic via DHL Express. Samples include full batch Certificate of Analysis (CoA) documentation for laboratory and sensory vetting.

5. What export documentation is provided for import clearance into Dominican Republic? +

Every commercial export shipment to Dominican Republic includes an official Phytosanitary Certificate issued by the Indonesian Agricultural Quarantine Agency (Barantin), batch-specific Certificate of Analysis (CoA), Certificate of Origin (COO / Form AK / Form D), Commercial Invoice, Packing List, and AWB/BL shipping documents.

6. What packaging formats are utilized to protect vanilla beans during international transit? +

Vanilla pods are vacuum-sealed in 1,000g heavy-duty multilayer food-grade barrier pouches that block oxygen and light. Pouches are packed into double-walled 10kg or 25kg export corrugated master cartons with interior moisture liners and tamper-evident strapping.

7. What Incoterms and shipping methods are supported for orders delivered to Dominican Republic? +

We support CIF to major international airports and seaports in Dominican Republic, CFR, and FOB Jakarta (Soekarno-Hatta CGK or Port of Tanjung Priok IDTPP) with complete cargo insurance.

8. What are your accepted international payment methods and banking terms? +

For air cargo orders (10kg - 250kg), payment is processed via Telegraphic Transfer (T/T). For full container loads (FCL) and large ocean contracts, we accept Irrevocable Letter of Credit at sight (L/C at sight) confirmed by recognized international commercial banks.

9. Are your vanilla beans traceable and compliant with European Union Deforestation Regulation (EUDR)? +

Yes. Our beans are harvested from regenerative agroforestry shade-tree farms in Sukabumi, West Java. We maintain polygon geo-location mapping for partner farmer cooperatives, guaranteeing 100% zero-deforestation and full EUDR compliance.

10. What is the guaranteed shelf life and recommended storage protocol for vacuum-packed beans? +

When maintained unopened in our heavy-duty vacuum foil packaging in a dark, cool environment (18°C–22°C with 50-60% relative humidity), our gourmet vanilla beans maintain prime aromatic quality, moisture, and vanillin levels for 24 to 36 months.

11. Can you supply customized vanilla powder mesh sizes or private label packaging? +

Yes. Our 100% pure vanilla powder is milled to 50–150 micron standards and can be packaged into 500g pouches, 1kg barrier bags, 5kg/10kg airtight pails, or 25kg fiber drums with customized private labeling (OEM) tailored to buyer specifications.
