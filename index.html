Frequently Asked Questions

Export Procurement Answers

Essential answers for international buyers and procurement directors.

What is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?
Our minimum order is 10 kg for air freight, making it accessible for craft breweries and mid-size bakeries. For sea freight (LCL), we recommend a minimum of 100 kg to optimize shipping costs. FCL container contracts are available for multi-ton manufacturing needs.
Can I request a sample before a bulk order?
Absolutely. B2B procurement requires quality assurance. We provide 50g - 200g samples with full chromatography analysis. Buyers are only required to cover express international courier shipping costs (via DHL/FedEx), which is fully credited on your first commercial order.
What is the typical Vanillin content of your Indonesian Planifolia?
Indonesian Planifolia is known for high yields, typically ranging between 1.5% to 2.5% vanillin content depending on harvest maturity and batch curing. Certificate of Analysis (CoA) verified via HPLC is provided with each commercial shipment.
Ethical & Eco-Responsible Trade

Our Sustainability Commitment

We believe world-class vanilla begins with healthy soils, direct farmer prosperity, and fully transparent harvest practices. Every vanilla bean is ethically sourced and naturally cured.

Direct Farmer Equity

We partner directly with smallholder farming cooperatives in West Java and beyond, guaranteeing above-market pricing and eliminating predatory middlemen.

100% Sun-Cured

Zero artificial heat lamps, zero chemical drying agents. Our pods undergo traditional sun-sweating and slow conditioning for authentic aromatic complexity.

Agroforestry System

Vanilla vines flourish under living support shade trees. This preserves canopy cover, prevents soil erosion, and maintains regional biodiversity.

Full Traceability

Every export batch is assigned a unique tracking lot number, documenting harvest date, cooperative origin, and vanillin analysis for complete transparency.
