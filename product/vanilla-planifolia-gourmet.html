Direct Procurement FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do the 14-15cm, 16-17cm, and 18-19cm lengths differ in quality? +

All three size brackets share identical Grade A chemical and moisture standards (1.8%-2.5% vanillin and 25%-35% moisture). The 16-17cm size is the most widely adopted international commercial benchmark, while the 18-19cm provides higher caviar volume per pod for visual display in fine pastry and gourmet retail tubes.

Can we request an evaluation sample prior to bulk commercial orders? +

Yes. We offer commercial evaluation sample kits (50g to 500g) dispatched via DHL Express with complete batch CoA documentation. Contact our sales desk to arrange sample delivery.

What are your accepted international payment terms? +

For air cargo shipments, we accept Telegraphic Transfer (T/T). For full container loads (FCL) and large ocean cargo contracts, we accept Irrevocable Letter of Credit (L/C at sight) confirmed by top-tier international banking institutions.
