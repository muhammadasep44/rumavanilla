Economic Justice

Direct Farmer Equity & Living Income Guarantee

In conventional spice trading, smallholder farmers often receive less than 15% of the export value, forced to sell unripe beans early to predatory middlemen to survive between harvests.

PT. Bumi Ruma Nusantara disrupts this destructive cycle. We work directly with farmer cooperatives, bypassing broker layers. We pay a living-income premium and guarantee purchase contracts before flowering, allowing farmers to let beans reach full maturity on the vine.

The Fair Sourcing Difference

  • No Broker Deductions: 100% of purchase payments go directly to cooperative family bank accounts.
  • Late Harvest Incentive: Higher bonuses paid for beans harvested at 8-9 months full maturity.
  • Community Development Fund: A portion of annual export profit is reinvested in clean rural water and cooperative curing sheds.

Ecological Agroforestry

Cultivated in Symbiosis with Living Shade Trees

Unlike monoculture crops that require clear-cutting of rainforests, vanilla orchids (*Vanilla Planifolia*) are climbing epiphyte vines that require living support trees to thrive.

Our partner farmers plant vanilla vines along living tutor trees like Gliricidia sepium (Gamal) and native fruit trees. This preserves canopy cover, provides natural leaf mulch, and creates thriving micro-habitats for birds and pollinators.

Canopy Preserved Zero Slash & Burn Natural Pest Barrier

Agroforestry Ecological Benefits

  • Soil Erosion Prevention: Mountain slopes retain stability through dense living root structures.
  • Natural Nitrogen Fixation: Gliricidia support trees fix atmospheric nitrogen into the soil, eliminating chemical fertilizers.
  • Carbon Sequestration: Perennial multi-tier agroforestry plots sequester significant carbon compared to open-sun monoculture.
Clean Cultivation

Zero Synthetic Pesticides or Fertilizers

Pure agricultural integrity protecting both human health and delicate soil biology.

Organic Compost Mulching

Farms utilize decomposing fallen leaves, coconut coir, and aged animal compost to provide balanced organic nutrition and maintain root moisture.

Botanical Pest Deterrents

Pests are managed with organic neem oil sprays, garlic decoctions, and beneficial predatory insect habitats instead of synthetic organophosphates.

Mountain Spring Hydration

Farms are hydrated using clean gravity-fed mountain spring water and natural monsoon rains, free from industrial chemical run-off or heavy metals.

Zero Fossil Fuels

Powered by 100% Equatorial Sun & Natural Convection

In some commercial origins, industrial dehydrators and fossil fuel-powered ovens are used to rapidly bake vanilla beans in days. This destroys delicate aroma fractions and consumes massive amounts of electrical power.

At PT. Bumi Ruma Nusantara, curing relies 100% on the natural warmth of the Indonesian sun and passive shade airflow. Our beans dry slowly over 3 months, achieving superior aromatic depth with near-zero industrial carbon emissions.

Social Equity

Empowering Female Agricultural Leaders in Sukabumi

Women play the central role in our vanilla ecosystem. Beyond hand-pollination, women lead bean grading, sorting, and daily sun-sweating monitoring at our Sukabumi facilities.

By providing formal contracts, leadership roles, and financial literacy workshops, we help female farmers build sovereign savings, fund children's higher education, and lead cooperative committees.

Sustainability FAQ

Ethical Trade Inquiries

Common questions from enterprise buyers and corporate ESG auditing teams.

Can you provide ESG audit documentation for our corporate compliance team?
Yes. We provide complete supplier questionnaires, labor policy declarations, zero-deforestation statements, and farm GPS polygon coordinates to fulfill your corporate ESG reporting and supply chain transparency standards.
How do you verify that farmgate premiums actually reach smallholders?
We execute purchase agreements directly with cooperative leadership with posted transparent base prices. Payments are settled through traceable direct transfers and cooperative receipt registries that are open to inspection by visiting international buyers.
What measures ensure zero child labor on contracted farms?
All cooperative members sign mandatory human rights agreements in compliance with ILO Conventions No. 138 & 182. Our field agronomists conduct frequent unannounced visits to farms during harvest periods to ensure children are in school and not engaged in hazardous labor.
