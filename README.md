## 📑 Pages & Architecture

1. **`index.html`** (Home) - Strategic sourcing advantages, commercial grades overview, technical data, customs clearance, shipping capabilities, sample program, and RFQ form.
2. **`about.html`** (About Us) - Corporate heritage, vision & mission, West Java terroir, smallholder farmer cooperatives, 4-stage curing process, climate-controlled warehousing, lab CoA testing, and regulatory certifications.
3. **`products.html`** (Products & Grades) - Vanilla Planifolia Gourmet (14-15cm, 16-17cm, 18-19cm), Tahitian Vanilla (16-17cm), Pure Vanilla Powder (Customizable Packaging, 50-150 micron), analytical chemistry profile (vanillin, 4-HBA, vanillic acid), master specification table, and heavy-gauge vacuum packaging.
4. **`sustainability.html`** (Sustainability & ESG) - 4-Pillar ESG framework, living income farmer premiums (+20-30%), agroforestry shade tree canopies, zero synthetic chemicals, EUDR compliance, and UN SDGs alignment.
5. **`contact.html`** (Contact & RFQ Desk) - Interactive B2B Request for Quotation (RFQ) form with dual routing (automated WhatsApp direct link & RFC mailto handler), corporate location, banking terms (T/T & L/C), Incoterms (FOB/CIF/CFR/EXW), and facility audit scheduling.
