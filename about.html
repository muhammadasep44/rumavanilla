Terroir & Origin

Why Indonesian Planifolia Stands Out in Global Industry

Unlike Bourbon vanilla from Madagascar which emphasizes floral-creamy notes, Indonesian Planifolia is world-renowned for its robust, warm, smoky, and balsamic depth.

This distinctive sensory profile makes Indonesian vanilla exceptionally heat-stable during commercial baking, distillation, and brewing. For extract makers, its high natural vanillin concentration (1.5% to 2.5%) yields a higher volume of premium pure vanilla extract per kilogram.

Curing Tradition

The 4-Stage Artisanal Curing Process

Converting green orchids into aromatic black pods requires months of patient craftsmanship.

1

Scalding (Killing)

Freshly hand-picked mature pods are immersed in hot water (65°C) for several minutes to halt vegetative growth and trigger glucovanillin enzymatic activity.

2

Sun Sweating

Pods are spread on cotton blankets under direct midday sun, then rolled tightly into wooden sweating boxes overnight to build internal heat and develop signature brown coloration.

3

Slow Shade Drying

Beans are transferred to well-ventilated bamboo racks in covered curing sheds for 4 to 8 weeks, slowly reaching target moisture levels (25-30% Grade A, 15-20% Grade B).

4

Conditioning & Sorting

Pods rest in waxed wooden conditioning boxes for 2 months to stabilize aroma compounds before hand-measuring, grading by length, and vacuum sealing.

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Essential information for corporate buyers evaluating our export credentials.

Where is PT. Bumi Ruma Nusantara located and can we visit?
Our corporate office and curing consolidation facilities are in Sukabumi, West Java, Indonesia. We warmly welcome pre-arranged visits by international commercial buyers, flavor chemists, and sourcing directors to tour our processing facilities and cooperative farm plots.
What commodities does your company specialize in?
Our core commodity is Indonesian Planifolia Vanilla Beans (Grade A Gourmet and Grade B Extraction Grade). We also facilitate export consolidation for premium Indonesian Cloves, Black Pepper, and Lucky Bamboo.
How does PT. Bumi Ruma Nusantara guarantee vanillin consistency?
We only harvest beans once the tips show golden yellow maturity (preventing premature harvesting). Every batch is cured through slow traditional sun sweating and verified with high-performance liquid chromatography (HPLC) testing prior to shipment.
What Incoterms and ports of departure do you support?
We routinely ship under FOB (Port of Tanjung Priok Jakarta / Tanjung Perak Surabaya), CIF to any designated worldwide port, CFR, and EXW for clients with their own freight forwarders. Air freight departs via Soekarno-Hatta International Airport (CGK).
