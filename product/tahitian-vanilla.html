Direct Procurement FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Tahitian vanilla be used for traditional vanilla extract? +

Yes, Tahitian vanilla produces an exquisite, aromatic single-origin extract with a pronounced floral and cherry profile. It is especially coveted by specialty extract makers and craft liquor distilleries looking for an artisan alternative to traditional Bourbon extracts.

How is Tahitian vanilla cured compared to Planifolia? +

Unlike Planifolia, Tahitian vanilla does not split open when fully ripe on the vine. We harvest beans at complete physiological maturity and apply a milder sweat-and-sun curing curve to carefully conserve volatile anisyl and heliotropin aromatic compounds.

What is the shelf life in unopened vacuum packaging? +

When stored in an unopened vacuum pack in a cool, dark room (18°C-22°C), our Tahitian vanilla pods maintain optimal moisture, aroma, and essential oil levels for 24 to 36 months.
